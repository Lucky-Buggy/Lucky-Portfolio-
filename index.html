Buka-Bus — Bus Booking System

The Challenge

Bus terminals relied on a fully manual booking process — passengers lined up at counters, staff tracked seat availability on paper or basic spreadsheets, and ticket references were handwritten. This led to overbooking, long queues, and no reliable record of transactions.

The Solution

Buka-Bus digitizes the entire booking flow into a web application. Passengers can view trip schedules, pick their own seats, and receive a confirmed booking instantly, removing manual counter work and reducing human error.

Key Features

  • Interactive seat selection map with real-time availability
  • Automated generation of unique booking reference tickets
  • Trip and schedule management for administrators

My Technical Role

I designed and normalized the database schema (trips, seats, bookings, passengers) to eliminate redundant data and preserve integrity, and built the backend booking logic in PHP — including seat-lock handling to prevent double-booking and reference number generation on successful transactions.
